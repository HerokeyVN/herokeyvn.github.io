# Visual review

## Reference principles

The explorations extract compositional principles rather than copying surface details:

- **Daksh Gajjar:** type behaves as architecture; the red line gains depth by passing behind and in front of selected glyphs.
- **Pier Gorelli:** an editorial grid, measured rules, and technical metadata make a restrained composition feel precise rather than empty.
- **Daoism Systems:** one spatial center creates hierarchy; surrounding typography and motion support that center instead of competing with it.

## Concept 01 — Typographic Monolith

- **Typography:** the identity is the dominant structure, split across two compressed lines rather than treated as a conventional headline.
- **Balance and negative space:** the dense center is held between a quiet technical header and a restrained professional footer.
- **Thread:** separate back and front paths cross selected parts of the wordmark, then exit into the next section without touching the role copy or navigation.
- **Depth:** outline guides and the circular counter stage sit behind the wordmark; the short foreground thread segments create the front plane.
- **Readability:** professional role and backend focus remain isolated from the expressive identity treatment. Mobile uses an intentional edge crop while keeping the full identity recoverable across the two-line composition.
- **Transition:** the black identity field gives way to an ivory statement about systems behind interfaces; the thread continues as a single right-side gesture.
- **Future 3D:** a compact service sculpture would remain nested in the wordmark counter and must never resize or displace the typography.

## Concept 02 — Engineering Ledger

- **Typography:** `BACK / END` is both profession and composition, while `HerokeyVN` remains explicit in the masthead.
- **Balance and negative space:** a heavy typographic block is counterweighted by a bounded inspection window and a generous paper field.
- **Thread:** the path is manually aligned to the title and inspection frame, using the frame as a purposeful pause rather than drawing across body content.
- **Depth:** rules, registration marks, type, and the inspection volume form a controlled set of shallow technical planes.
- **Readability:** this is the most literal backend identity of the three; metadata, title, description, and CTA each occupy protected grid cells.
- **Transition:** the paper ledger folds into a black systems statement, preserving the vertical grid and red narrative cue.
- **Future 3D:** the framed inspection window provides a strict camera and performance budget for one system artifact.

## Concept 03 — Spatial Aperture

- **Typography:** `HERO / KEYVN` surrounds a central void, mixing solid and outline glyphs so identity and focal volume share one composition.
- **Balance and negative space:** the aperture is the gravity center; peripheral copy is sparse and anchored to the lower edge.
- **Thread:** a tailored orbit enters through the upper word, passes behind the central type/aperture layer, and returns toward the role without crossing it.
- **Depth:** concentric fields, outline glyphs, and the masked thread create spatial depth before any real-time 3D is introduced.
- **Readability:** the identity treatment is expressive, while `Backend Developer` and the service description remain direct and high contrast.
- **Transition:** the central field expands into a darker violet systems section, maintaining the same spatial axis across the fold.
- **Future 3D:** the aperture becomes a fixed projection volume for one low-motion backend system object; the layout does not adapt around arbitrary geometry.
